# KINLOOP Demo Script — HBS Pitch Flow

## Setup

1. Log in as the demo user (Jenn)
2. Ensure Mia's profile is loaded (age 4y 2mo, allergies: peanuts)
3. Have the following ready to paste:
   - A sample school email (field trip permission slip)
   - A YouTube URL of a kids' activity video
   - A question about bedtime tantrums

## Flow (5 minutes)

### Opening (30 seconds)

> "Meet Jenn. She has a 4-year-old named Mia. Every week, Jenn gets 10+ emails from school, tracks doctor visits on paper, and saves activity ideas to a bookmark folder she never opens. KINLOOP turns all of that chaos into one intelligent dashboard."

Show the 2x2 dashboard grid. Point out the four quadrants.

### Quadrant 1: Scheduler (90 seconds)

> "Jenn just got this email from Mia's teacher about a field trip."

1. Navigate to Scheduler
2. Paste the school email
3. Click "Extract with AI"
4. Show the extracted events, action items, and payment ($15 due May 10)
5. Show the suggested reply draft
6. Click "Approve" — event appears in the calendar

> "In 3 seconds, Claude extracted 2 events, 5 action items, and a $15 payment. No more missed permission slips."

### Quadrant 3: Play Lab (60 seconds)

> "Jenn saw a fun activity on YouTube. Instead of bookmarking it and forgetting..."

1. Navigate to Play Lab
2. Paste the YouTube URL
3. Show the extracted activity card: title, materials list, steps, age check
4. Point out the Amazon/Target search links for materials
5. Show the age-appropriateness check (Mia is 4, activity is for ages 3-6)

> "Claude watched the video, extracted a step-by-step plan, and even generated a shopping list."

### Quadrant 4: Coach (60 seconds)

> "At bedtime, Mia has been having tantrums about brushing teeth."

1. Navigate to Coach
2. Type: "Mia has been having tantrums at bedtime about brushing teeth. What should we try?"
3. Show the response with evidence-based strategies
4. Point out the source citations (Whole-Brain Child, How to Talk So Kids Will Listen)

> "The coach knows Mia is 4, knows her context, and gives advice grounded in real parenting books — not generic internet tips."

### The Moat (30 seconds)

> "Here is what makes KINLOOP different: every quadrant shares the same child profile. The Coach knows about the upcoming field trip. The Scheduler knows about Mia's peanut allergy. The Play Lab knows her exact age. This is cross-quadrant intelligence — and it is the moat."

### Close (30 seconds)

> "KINLOOP is not a chatbot. It is not a calendar. It is the first AI-native operating system for parenting. We are launching with 50 beta families this summer."

## Backup Demo

If live AI calls fail, the seed data provides pre-loaded examples in every quadrant. Navigate to each page to show the seeded content.
